---
title: "Deactivate Payout Destination"
description: "Stop payouts to a destination without deleting its history."
api: "DELETE /v1/payout-destinations/by-reference/{reference}"
---

Stops payouts to a destination. No new split will land somewhere the payee has disowned.

Requires the `X-API-Key` header with a Payments capability key.

<Note>
  **This deactivates; it does not delete.** The record survives because your past payments reference it for their settlement history. Destroying it would destroy the record of where money already went.
</Note>

<Note>
  **It is idempotent.** Deactivating an already-inactive destination succeeds.
</Note>

## Path parameters

<ParamField path="reference" type="string" required>
  Your own id for the payee, as you sent it at creation.
</ParamField>

## Response

<ResponseField name="success" type="boolean" />

<ResponseField name="provider_warning" type="string">
  Appears when the destination was deactivated here but the provider could not be reached to do the same. The destination is off as far as mgPass is concerned; the warning tells you to check the provider. It is still a success — an operator who asked to stop payouts is never told nothing happened when something did.
</ResponseField>

## Errors

| Status | Code | When |
|--------|------|------|
| 401 | `unauthorized` | Missing or invalid `X-API-Key`. |
| 404 | `not_found` | No payout destination with that reference. |
