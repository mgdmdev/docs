---
title: "FedCM Client Metadata"
api: "GET /fedcm/client-metadata"
description: "Returns the privacy policy and terms of service URLs for the requesting client."
---

The terms and privacy links the browser shows in its own consent UI, taken from the Application's branding.

<Note>
  Called by the browser, not by your code. Set `terms_url` and `privacy_url` in your Application's branding and they appear here; leave them unset and users see the mgPass defaults.
</Note>

## Headers

<ParamField header="Sec-Fetch-Dest" type="string" required>
  Must be `webidentity`.
</ParamField>

## Query parameters

<ParamField query="client_id" type="string">
  The relying party's `application_id`. An empty object is returned when it is absent.
</ParamField>

## Response

<ResponseField name="privacy_policy_url" type="string">
  From the Application's `branding.privacy_url`, falling back to `https://mgpass.net/privacy`.
</ResponseField>

<ResponseField name="terms_of_service_url" type="string">
  From the Application's `branding.terms_url`, falling back to `https://mgpass.net/terms`.
</ResponseField>

## Errors

| Status | Code | When |
|--------|------|------|
| 400 | `invalid_request` | The `Sec-Fetch-Dest: webidentity` header was absent. |
