## What mgPass is

mgPass is an identity, payments, subscriptions, and rewards platform. You register one [Application](/guides/applications) and attach the capabilities you need.

Identity is the foundation every integration starts from. Payments and subscriptions are what most people are here to build. Rewards is one of the things you can attach.

A capability is enabled on an Application. Sign-in uses the OAuth capability. Payments, subscriptions, and rewards each authenticate with that capability's key in the `X-API-Key` header.

A payment is not a subscription. A ticket, an order, a top-up — you send the amount, we charge it. Recurring billing is the [Subscriptions](/guides/subscriptions/overview) capability, and it does not cover those.

Mobile money is a first-class way to pay, not a fallback. Most Ghanaian buyers pay that way.
