## What is the Developer Console?

The **Developer Console** at [console.mgpass.net](https://console.mgpass.net) is the self-serve portal for building on mgPass. It replaces the old "email MG Digital to provision an Application" hand-off: you sign up, create your own [Applications](/guides/applications), enable the capabilities you need (OAuth, Rewards, Subscriptions), and wire up webhooks — all without waiting on a human.

The console is a separate surface from the consumer-facing account portal at `mgpass.net`. It signs you in through the standard mgPass OAuth flow and scopes everything you see to the organizations and applications **you own**.

<Note>
The console talks to the same API and the same `application_id` spine described in [Applications](/guides/applications). Anything you create here is a first-class Application — identical to one provisioned by an admin. There is no "developer-tier" second-class object.
</Note>

## Sign up

<Steps>
  <Step title="Create a developer account">
    Go to [auth.mgpass.net/developer/signup](https://auth.mgpass.net/developer/signup) and register with your work email. This is the same identity system end-users sign in with, but the `developer` intent also grants you the `mgpass:developer` role.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Verify your email">
    Click the verification link we email you. Verification is required before any `developer:*` scopes are granted.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Land in the console">
    After verifying you're redirected to [console.mgpass.net](https://console.mgpass.net). The console performs an OAuth code-grant against `auth.mgpass.net/authorize`, and your session carries the `developer:applications:read`, `developer:applications:write`, and `developer:webhooks:write` scopes.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Create an application

From the console dashboard, **New Application** creates an Application spine row owned by your organization. You provide a display name and slug; the slug must be unique across mgPass (you'll get a friendly conflict message if it's taken).

Every application you create is stamped with `created_by = your user` and bound to your organization. Ownership is enforced on every request — you can only read or modify applications your organization owns. Attempting to reach another developer's `application_id` returns `403`.

## Enable capabilities

An Application does nothing until you attach a capability. In the console, open your Application → **Capabilities** and enable the ones you need:

  </Card>
  <Card title="Rewards" icon="gift" href="/guides/awarding-points">
    Emit events that earn users points in the shared loyalty ledger.

  <Card title="Subscriptions" icon="credit-card" href="/guides/subscriptions/overview">

Enabling a capability inserts its row; disabling it deletes the row. Presence of the row *is* the on/off switch — there is no separate boolean.

### One-time secret reveal

When you enable a capability that issues a credential (the OAuth `client_secret`, or a Rewards/Subscriptions API key) **or rotate one**, the plaintext value is shown **exactly once**, immediately after the action.

<Warning>
Copy the secret as soon as it's revealed. mgPass stores only a hash — we cannot show it to you again. If you lose it, your only recovery is to rotate the credential, which immediately invalidates the old one. Store secrets in a vault or your platform's secret manager, never in source control.
</Warning>

## Set up webhooks

In the console, open your Application → **Webhooks** → **Add endpoint**. You provide:

- **Endpoint URL** — your HTTPS endpoint that receives `POST` payloads.
- **Event types** — which events to subscribe to (across any capability the Application has enabled).

On creation the console reveals the **signing secret once** (same one-time-reveal rule as above) and fires a **test ping** so you can confirm your endpoint is reachable before any real event flows. Verify the `X-Webhook-Signature` HMAC on every delivery — see [Webhooks](/guides/webhooks) for the verification recipe. Failed deliveries are retried with backoff, and you can inspect or replay them.

## Daily caps

To keep the self-serve surface safe, a few actions are rate-limited per developer per day. These are generous for normal integration work and reset at midnight UTC:

| Action | Daily cap |
|---|---|
| Capability enables | 3 / day |
| Credential (secret/key) rotations | 10 / day |
| Webhook endpoint creates | 10 / day |

Exceeding a cap returns `429` with a `Retry-After` header. If you have a legitimate need for higher limits, contact MG Digital.

<Note>
These caps govern self-serve console actions only. Runtime traffic (token issuance, checkout, rewards events) is governed separately by your application's plan — see the per-capability guides.
</Note>
